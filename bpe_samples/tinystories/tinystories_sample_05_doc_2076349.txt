One day, the boy's mom said he could go outside and play. He was very excited, and he ran outside as fast as he could. He had so much fun with his friends and he forgot the time. It was dark when he realized he had to go home.
The boy was scared, because it was dark and he did not know how to get home. He looked for the light at his house, but it was not easy to see. He walked for a long time and finally, he saw the faint light shining from his home.
But when he got to his house, he found out that the truth was different. His mom was very sad, because she had been worried about him since he didn't come back. The little boy was sad, too, but he had learned an important lesson about not following the truth.
