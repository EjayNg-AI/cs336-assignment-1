Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a compassionate boy. He loved to help everyone. One day, Tim saw a man who looked sad. The man was holding a newspaper. The newspaper had a story that made the man feel bad. Tim wanted to help the man feel better.
Tim walked up to the man and asked, "Why do you hate the newspaper?" The man said, "The story makes me sad." Tim thought for a moment. He knew he had to help the man feel better.
Tim took the man's hand and said, "Let's do something fun together!" They went to the park and played on the swings. They laughed and had a great time. The man forgot about the newspaper and was happy again. Tim was glad he could help the man feel better.
