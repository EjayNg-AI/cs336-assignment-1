Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a wealthy man named Mr. Brown. He had a big store where he sold many things. Mr. Brown was a nice man with a kind spirit.
One day, a little girl named Lily came to the store. She wanted to buy a toy but did not have enough money. Mr. Brown saw her sad face and wanted to help. He gave her the toy for free and Lily was very happy.
The next day, something unexpected happened. Mr. Brown found a big box outside his store. Inside the box were many beautiful toys. A note said, "Thank you for your kind spirit. Love, Lily." It turns out that Lily's family made toys and wanted to share them with Mr. Brown. Now, his store had even more things to sell, and he became even wealthier.
