Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a very lazy cat. This cat liked to sleep all day and never wanted to play. One day, while the cat was napping, a tiny flea came to visit him.
The flea jumped on the lazy cat and started to bite. The cat woke up and felt itchy. He tried to catch the flea, but the flea was too fast. The cat chased the flea all day long, but he could not catch it. He was very tired and wanted to quit.
Finally, the lazy cat asked his friend, a wise old owl, for help. The owl told the cat to take a bath in the river. The cat did as the owl said, and the flea quit biting him. The lazy cat learned that sometimes, you have to work hard to solve a problem. And from that day on, the cat was not so lazy anymore.
