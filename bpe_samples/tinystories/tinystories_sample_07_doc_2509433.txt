One morning, Tom the cat woke up very early. He saw the sun coming up and the sky was turning from black to blue. Tom was so happy that he wanted to play with his friend, Sam the dog.
Tom went to Sam's house and said, "Sam, wake up! Let's play outside!" Sam was still sleepy, but he got up and went to play with Tom. They played hide and seek, and Tom hid in a big black box. When Sam found him, the box fell over and made a loud noise.
All their friends heard the noise and came to see what happened. Tom felt very embarrassed because he made such a loud noise. He said, "I'm sorry, I didn't mean to make so much noise." Sam told him, "It's okay, Tom. We all make mistakes."
The moral of the story is that it's okay to make mistakes and feel embarrassed sometimes, as long as we learn from them and say sorry.
