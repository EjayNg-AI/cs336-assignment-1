Once upon a time, in a lovely little house, there lived a girl named Lily and her dog, Max. They were best friends and loved to play in the garden. One day, Lily's friend, Tom, came over to play with them. Tom liked to tease Max by taking his ball and running away.
"Stop teasing Max!" Lily said to Tom. "It's not nice to tease others."
Tom didn't listen and kept teasing Max. This made Max sad and he didn't want to play anymore. Lily felt bad for Max and told Tom, "If you don't stop teasing, you won't be allowed to play with us."
Tom finally stopped teasing Max and said sorry. They all played together and had a great time. They learned that teasing others is a waste of time and being kind brings more fun.
From that day on, Tom never teased Max again. They all became good friends and always played nicely together. The moral of the story is to treat others with kindness and respect.
