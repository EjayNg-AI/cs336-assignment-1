Once upon a time, there was a compassionate boy named Tim. Tim had a dog named Max. Max was big and liked to play outside. Tim wanted to take Max for a walk, so he got Max's leash.
Tim and Max started walking. They were having fun. Then, something unexpected happened. Max saw a cat and ran fast. Max pulled Tim and the leash came off!
Tim was sad and scared. He looked for Max, but Max was gone. Then, Tim saw Max behind a tree. Max had found a lost little dog. Tim put the leash on Max and the little dog. They all went home together. Tim's mom was so happy. Tim had completed his walk and found a new friend.
