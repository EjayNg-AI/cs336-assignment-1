Once upon a time, in a small town, there were two friends, Tom and Tim. They loved to play games. They were competitive and always wanted to win. One day, they found a new way to play. They decided to have a race.
The race was to see who could pinch the most leaves from the big tree in the park. They ran as fast as they could to the tree. Tom pinched one leaf, and then Tim pinched one leaf too. They kept pinching leaves, trying to be the fastest.
Soon, they both had many leaves in their hands. They ran back to the start to count the leaves. Tom had ten leaves, and Tim had nine leaves. Tom was the winner! They laughed and had fun, even though there was a little conflict in their game. In the end, they were still the best of friends.
