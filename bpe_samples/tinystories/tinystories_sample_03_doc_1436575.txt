Once upon a time, in a far away land, there was a king. The king had a very big and shiny crown. He loved his crown very much. One day, he saw a different crown in a shop. This crown was not as big, but it was very pretty. The king wanted to have this different crown too.
The king went to the shop and asked the man, "Can you supply me with that different crown?" The man said, "Yes, but you must be kind to all people in your land." The king thought about it and said, "I can do that."
The king started to be kind to everyone. He gave food to the hungry and helped the sick. One day, a little girl came to the king. She said, "I lost my toy. Can you help me find it?" The king helped the girl find her toy. The girl was very happy and said, "Thank you, king."
The man from the shop saw the king being kind. He gave the king the different crown. The king put it on and felt very proud. But then, something unexpected happened. The big and shiny crown fell off his head and broke. The king was sad, but he realized that being kind was more important than having a big crown.
The moral of the story is that being kind to others is more important than having many things.
