Once upon a time, there was a hairy dog named Spot. Spot was a very good dog. He lived in a small house with his friend, a girl named Sue.
One day, Sue and Spot went to a park. At the park, they saw a big steel slide. Spot and Sue were very happy. They wanted to play on the slide.
But the slide was dirty. Sue took a cloth and began to wipe the slide. Spot helped her. They wiped and wiped until the slide was clean. Then, Spot and Sue played on the slide all day. They were very happy and had lots of fun.
